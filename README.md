## 🤔 Why Zametki?

Most note-taking apps sync your data to the cloud by default. Zametki takes a different approach: **your notes stay on your device**. Built specifically for privacy-first, it simplicity, offline access, and zero distractions. No accounts, no subscriptions, no background servers. Just a clean, fast interface to capture lectures, ideas, and everyday tasks whenever and wherever you need them.

✅ **100% client-side storage** – notes are saved directly in your browser  
🔒 **Private by design** – nothing leaves your device  
🌐 **Works offline** – no internet connection required  
👤 **User-friendly** – lightweight, fast, and distraction-free  

## 👀 How can I use it?

Zametki runs entirely in your browser, so setup is instant:

1. **Open the app** – visit the "LINK PLACEHOLDER" link.
2. **Start writing** – create, edit, and organize your notes.
3. **Save automatically** – all changes are stored instantly in your local database storage.
4. **Access anytime** – reload the page or work offline; your notes will be right where you left them.
